Ordering

How to Order

  1. 01
    Flag the Summer Incentive

    Tell Orders you’re using the Summer Incentive on the order. It won’t be applied automatically.

  2. 02
    Spell out both builds

    Clearly state what the customer is paying for and what they should receive.

  3. 03
    One DAC upgrade per Selekt DSM

    Even when a system has multiple DACs, only one DAC moves up a level.

  4. 04
    Dual mono Organik

    Order a stereo Organik Selekt DSM with a stereo Line Out cartridge and the customer receives 2 × Organik Mono DAC. They pay the difference for 2 × Mono Line Out cartridges.

Who to call first

Start with the warmest opportunities.

There’s been no notice period on this one. It arrives straight after 343, and it’s a shorter campaign, so build a prospect list, book demos and start conversations now.

  • 01
    Selekt DSM considerersCustomers already looking at Selekt DSM who haven’t committed yet.
  • 02
    Great speakers, weaker sourceCustomers with good speakers and amplifiers but a less impressive source. A good demo will encourage them to make the step up.
  • 03
    Older Linn streamersExisting Linn customers with older streamers, particularly Akurate or Majik DSM.

The demo is key. Let customers hear the difference between DAC levels, and show the long-term value of Selekt’s upgradeable platform.

Floor stock

Hear it to sell it.

The upgrade is easiest to sell when customers can hear it. A Selekt DSM on the floor with cartridges and DACs ready to swap turns “what’s the difference?” into a ten-minute listening session.

+5%

Extra floor-stock allowance. Commit to floor stock in any Selekt DSM, cartridge or DAC between 16 November 2026 and 5 January 2027 and we’ll add a further 5% on top of your standard floor-stock allowance.

  • Keep at least two DAC levels on hand so customers can compare Standard, Katalyst and Organik on the same system.
  • Demo with the customer’s own music, and on speakers close to what they own.
  • Show the upgrade path: today’s DAC choice isn’t the last one they’ll make.
  • The Summer Incentive upgrade applies to customer orders, not demo purchases. The extra 5% is how floor stock is rewarded.
